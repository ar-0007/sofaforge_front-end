# 3D model credits

`sectional-sofa.glb`

- Title: **Low Poly Sectional Couch**
- Author: Stephen White
- Source: [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/2ntmr7Oka5z) — the Google Poly archive
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

Downloaded unmodified and renamed. 510 triangles, 74 kB, no textures, so it is
on screen inside one frame.

It was chosen over the photoreal alternatives on weight and on shape: the
Khronos velvet sofa is 3 MB and is not a sectional, and the nearest photoreal
sectionals found were 21–31 MB, which needs a Draco/meshopt pipeline before it
belongs in a hero.

Materials are re-authored at runtime rather than edited in the file — `mat5` is
the upholstery and takes the catalogue fabric colour, `mat18` is the legs. The
licence permits both the use and the remix; attribution is rendered on the page
itself, in the credit line under the fabric picker.
